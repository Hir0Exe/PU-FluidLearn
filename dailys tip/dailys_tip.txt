Listening (Auditory comprehension)
Listen to greetings: Look for "Hello" and "Goodbye" variations in songs.

Podcasts for beginners: Search for "English for absolute beginners" and listen for 5 minutes.

Identify numbers: Listen to a countdown and write the numbers you hear.

Background noise: Play English radio while you cook or clean; don't try to understand everything.

Listen for names: Watch a movie scene and write down all the names of the characters mentioned.

The rhythm of English: Focus on the "music" of the language, not the words.

Listen to the weather: Try to catch if it will be "sunny" or "rainy" in an English report.

Speaking (Oral expression)
Repeat the "Hello": Mimic the way a native speaker says "How are you?"

Record your voice: Say your name and where you are from, then listen to it.

Shadowing: Listen to a short sentence and repeat it immediately after.

Sing a chorus: Choose a simple pop song and sing the main part.

Self-talk: Describe what you are doing in one word (e.g., "Coffee," "Book," "Walk").

Practice the alphabet: Spell your name out loud in English.

Control your speed: Speak slowly; clarity is more important than speed at A1.

Reading (Reading comprehension)
Label your house: Put sticky notes on your "Door," "Window," and "Fridge."

Read children's books: They use simple vocabulary and lots of pictures.

Check the date: Look at your phone calendar in English every morning.

Read a menu: Look for food words you recognize (Pizza, Salad, Burger).

Follow a recipe: Try to read the ingredients of a simple dish in English.

Social media: Follow one English-learning account and read their "Word of the day."

Scan for cognates: Look for words that look like Spanish (e.g., "Family," "Problem").

Read signs: Look for "Exit," "Push," or "Pull" when you are out.

Writing (Written expression)
The Shopping List: Write your grocery list in English today.

Daily Emotion: Write one word about how you feel (e.g., "Happy," "Tired").

Copy a quote: Find a short English quote and write it in a notebook.

Write the colors: Look around and write 5 colors you see.

Simple intro: Write: "My name is [Name] and I am learning English."

Text a friend: Send a "Good morning" or "Have a nice day" in English.

Describe your pet: Write 3 words about your pet or a favorite animal.

Plan your day: Write three verbs for your tasks (e.g., "Work," "Eat," "Sleep").
